About this dashboard: Rebuilt daily from March to November and on the 1st of each month from December to February. NASA POWER and Open-Meteo climate data and iNaturalist citizen-science observations are fetched on each run, and so are Colorado West Nile virus case counts from CDC: finalized annual counts (ArboNET) and provisional weekly counts for the current season (NNDSS). The Lyme disease and county case tables are static values stored in this repository; they are not fetched and have not been verified. Risk levels are an experimental index: they have not been validated and are not probabilities of infection. Notebooks 04 and 05 use synthetic data for demonstration only. See the scientific audit for known limitations. For official guidance, see CDPHE West Nile virus, CDPHE tick-borne diseases and CDC. Built on the AEDES framework, originally developed for dengue surveillance in the Philippines (Ligot & Toledo, 2021).
ℹ️ About CDC data recency: CDC's finalized annual case counts are published approximately 12–18 months after the end of each calendar year. West Nile virus counts run to the latest year CDC has finalized; the Lyme table ends in 2024. The reliability table below shows the latest observation for each source. Current-season West Nile virus counts are provisional and by week of report; they are revised as late reports arrive. There is no in-season source for Lyme disease in Colorado. Reports that rely exclusively on finalized historical data are grouped in the Historical Archive section at the bottom of this page.
